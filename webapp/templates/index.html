The 2H

Toyota’s legendary 4.0L inline-six diesel, fitted across the 40, 60 and 70 Series LandCruisers, as well as Dyna trucks, Coaster buses, and even some Toyota forklifts. An engine from an era when diesels were built to work: simple, understressed, mechanically injected and extraordinarily durable. No timing belt, no timing chain, no ECU, no common rail, no DPF, no DEF, and no EGR. Just gears, iron and diesel. An engine that earned its reputation the hard way. It’s also the engine this whole archive really started around.
